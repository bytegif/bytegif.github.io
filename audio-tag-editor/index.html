FEATURES

Why Audio Tag Editor?

A focused utility designed for privacy-first music management, batch edits, and complete control over your audio metadata.

Offline & Private

Every edit is processed locally on your device. Your audio files and metadata stay on your phone.

Batch Editing

Edit album, artist, genre, cover art, and more across multiple songs simultaneously with a few taps.

Custom Album Art

Add, replace, and embed custom album artwork. Crop images to 1:1, stretch them, or save existing cover art to your gallery.

Comprehensive Metadata

Edit song title, artist, album, album artist, genre, composer, lyrics, comments, and track numbers in one place.

Select All

Quickly select all audio files in a folder or list with one tap for faster multi-file cleanup.

Folder Browser

Browse and manage audio files by folders with a directory explorer built for quick navigation.
