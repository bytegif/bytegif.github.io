FEATURES

Why Audio Tag Editor?

A simple yet powerful tool designed for privacy-first music management, batch edits, and complete control over your audio metadata.

100% Offline & Private

Every edit is processed locally on your device. Your audio files and metadata never leave your phone, ensuring complete privacy and security.

Batch Editing

Edit album, artist, genre, cover art, and more across multiple songs simultaneously with just a few taps.

Advanced Album Art

Add, replace, and embed custom album artwork. Crop images to 1:1, stretch them, or keep the original aspect ratio. Save existing cover art to your gallery too.

Comprehensive Metadata Editing

Edit song title, artist, album, album artist, genre, composer, lyrics, comments, and more without leaving the app.

Select All

Quickly select all audio files with one tap for faster multi-file editing and cleanup across your music library.

Organize Your Music

Browse and manage audio files by folders with an intuitive directory-based explorer built for quick access and organization.

Modern & Intuitive Design

Enjoy a clean, responsive interface built with Jetpack Compose, optimized for both light and dark themes.
