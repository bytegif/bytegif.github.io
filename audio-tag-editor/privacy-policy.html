Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Audio Tag Editor. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information related to you. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Audio Tag Editor is built with user privacy as a core tenet. Here is how your data is handled:

  • Audio Files and Metadata: All audio editing, file scanning, and metadata editing are performed completely offline, local to your device. We do not upload, store, or transmit your audio files or any metadata (such as titles, artists, or album covers) to external servers.
  • Device Permissions: The app requests storage/media permissions solely to read audio files from your device and save your modified tags back to the storage.

3. Third-Party Services (Advertising)

To keep this application free, we integrate advertisement services. These third-party providers may collect and process information about you to serve personalized or non-personalized ads:

  • Google AdMob: AdMob utilizes device identifiers, cookies, and other usage statistics to serve advertisements. You can learn more about how Google uses data by visiting their Advertising Policies.

4. Data Security

Since your files and metadata remain entirely on your device, the security of that data relies on your device's built-in operating system security settings. We implement standard programming best practices locally to ensure data integrity during file modification.

5. Children's Privacy

Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at bytegif@gmail.com.
