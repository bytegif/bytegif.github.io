FEATURES

Your devices, connected directly.

SendChat keeps conversations and transfers on your local network, with simple pairing and secure device-to-device connections.

Direct local connections

Connect devices on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot and transfer directly, without routing files through cloud storage.

End-to-end encryption

Secure sessions use key exchange and authenticated encryption to protect messages and file transfers between paired devices.

Automatic device discovery

Find SendChat devices on your network automatically, without having to look up or enter IP addresses.

Private peer-to-peer chat

Send messages between paired devices, with delivery confirmations and controls for managing conversations.

Send files and folders

Share photos, videos, documents, archives, or entire directories, and track progress as they transfer.

One app, many platforms

Connect Android phones, Macs, Windows PCs, and Linux computers with SendChat’s cross-platform apps.
