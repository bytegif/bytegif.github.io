FEATURES

Your devices, connected directly.

SendChat keeps chats and transfers on your local network, with native apps for desktop and mobile and a browser portal for nearby devices.

No cloud, no accounts

Connect over your local Wi-Fi or hotspot. Messages and files go directly between devices instead of through cloud storage or relay servers.

End-to-end encryption

Pair with a QR code or 6-digit PIN. Paired sessions use ECDH key agreement and AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption.

Automatic discovery & pairing

Find nearby devices with mDNS and UDP discovery, then verify a connection with a QR code or six-digit PIN—no IP address entry required.

Private peer-to-peer chat

Message paired SendChat devices and browser users, with delivery confirmations, copy-and-paste shortcuts, and conversation cleanup.

Share files and folders

Send photos, videos, documents, archives, and folders with live progress, pause and cancel controls, and desktop drag-and-drop.

One app, many platforms

Install the native app on Android, macOS, Windows, or Linux. iPhone, iPad, and other devices can connect through a browser.

Local Web Share

Open a local web portal and share its QR code or URL. Other devices on the same Wi-Fi can send messages and files or download shared files without installing an app.

Lightweight, with device controls

Compact Android builds and per-device notification settings help keep the app lean and notifications under your control.
