FEATURES

Your devices, connected directly.

Connect nearby devices directly, or use a browser for quick sharing without installing the app.

Direct local connections

Connect devices on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot and transfer directly, without routing files through cloud storage.

End-to-end encryption

Pair with a QR code or 6-digit PIN, then protect messages and file transfers with key exchange and authenticated encryption.

Automatic device discovery

Find SendChat devices on your network automatically, without having to look up or enter IP addresses.

Private peer-to-peer chat

Message paired devices with delivery confirmations, copy-and-paste shortcuts, and bulk conversation cleanup.

Send files and folders

Share photos, videos, documents, archives, or entire folders with live progress, pause and cancel controls, and desktop drag-and-drop.

One app, many platforms

Use native apps on Android, macOS, Windows, and Linux, with browser access for iPhone, iPad, and other devices.

Local Web Share

Show a QR code or local URL so any browser on the same Wi-Fi can send messages and files or download shared files—no app installation needed.

Per-device notification controls

Choose whether each connected device plays notification sounds or stays muted.
