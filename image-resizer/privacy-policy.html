Privacy Policy for Image Resizer

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Image Resizer ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect and Access

Image Resizer is primarily a local offline utility designed to respect your privacy. We do not collect or store your personal identity information on external servers.

A. Local Media and Storage Access

To provide image resizing, cropping, and compression features, the App requests permission to access photos and storage on your device:

  • Photo Library / Media Storage: Used only to let you select, edit, and save images within the App.
  • Local Storage: Edited images and app settings are stored locally on your device only.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep the App free, Image Resizer integrates third-party services that may collect anonymous device information for ad serving and analytics.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Google AdMob may collect and use device advertising identifiers (e.g., AAID), IP addresses, and interaction data to serve ads. For more information, please review:

  • Google Privacy & Terms Policy

3. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at bytegif@gmail.com.
