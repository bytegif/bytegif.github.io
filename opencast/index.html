FEATURES

Everything you need for podcasts and radio.

OpenCast combines podcast subscription management with global live radio streaming in one lightweight Android app.

Millions of Podcasts

Search and explore trending shows across every category, or paste any custom RSS feed URL to subscribe directly.

Worldwide Live Radio

Tune into thousands of live FM, AM, and web radio stations across countries and genres with instant streaming.

Cloud Sync & Guest Mode

Sync your subscriptions, history, playlists, and favorites across devices, or use local Guest Mode without an account.

Offline Downloads

Download episodes to device storage and listen anytime without using mobile data.

Smart Playlists & Queue

Create playlists, manage your playback queue, bookmark favorite radio stations, and leave episode comments.

Sleep Timer & Speed Control

Customize playback speed, set sleep timers to pause playback automatically, and control audio from the lock screen.
