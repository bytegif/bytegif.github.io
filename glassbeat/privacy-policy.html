Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the GlassBeat application (referred to as "the App" or "GlassBeat").

1. Information Collection and Use

GlassBeat is built as an offline-first local audio player. We prioritize your privacy and do not collect or transmit your personal data to external servers.

Local Data Access:

  • Media Library Access: The App requires permission to read your device's external storage (audio files) to display and play your local music library. This data remains strictly on your device.
  • Local Storage (Room Database): The App stores your listening history, custom playlists, and equalizer settings locally in an offline database. This data is never shared.

2. Third-Party Services (Advertising)

To keep the App free to use, we intend to integrate third-party advertising networks (specifically Google AdMob). These services may collect information used to identify your device, serve personalized ads, or track ad interactions.

You can read the privacy policies of our third-party provider below:

  • Google Play Services & AdMob Privacy Policy

3. Permissions Required

The App requests the following permissions to function correctly:

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: To scan and index your local audio files.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To show the playback controller notification panel.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: To keep audio playing smoothly in the background.

4. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at bytegif@gmail.com.
