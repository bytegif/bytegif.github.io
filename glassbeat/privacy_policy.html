Privacy Policy for GlassBeat

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GlassBeat ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect and Access

GlassBeat is primarily a local offline music player designed to respect your privacy. We do not collect or store your personal identity information on external servers.

A. Local Audio and Storage Access

To provide music playback and library features, the App requests permission to access audio files stored on your device:

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used exclusively to scan, organize, and play your local audio files.
  • Local Database (Room Storage): Favorites, playlists, listen history, and equalizer presets are stored locally on your device only.

B. Device and Performance Data

The App uses standard system features for background playback and notification controls:

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Keeps audio playback active when the App is in the background or screen is locked.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Displays playback controls on your lock screen and notification bar.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep the App free, GlassBeat integrates third-party services that may collect anonymous device information for ad serving and analytics.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Google AdMob may collect and use device advertising identifiers (e.g., AAID), IP address, and interaction data to serve ads. For more information, please review:

  • Google Privacy & Terms Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services

3. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at bytegif@gmail.com.
